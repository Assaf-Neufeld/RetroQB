# P7 verification — authored match coverage

September 29, 2026. P7.1–P7.4 implemented in Debug development mode after P6 commit `c8f2cae`. Plan/runtime versions: `defense-plan-p7-v1` / `defense-runtime-p7-v1`. Normal gameplay remains legacy until P10.

## Definitions and ownership

`def.cover3-match.v2` and `def.quarters-match.v2` introduce explicit functional roles, initial receiver ownership and authored release tables. Historical `def.cover3-match` remains the version-one Buzz spot call; historical `def.quarters-match` retains its legacy Quarters Zone meaning. `--engine match` exposes the new calls on keys 7/8 in both defensive session menus. Other development modes retain their existing catalogs. Recorded plans retain their original call identity and meaning.

Cover 3 Match locks outside verticals and carries #2 with the apex. A shallow #1/vertical #2 exchange commits as a complete chain or leaves both previous owners accountable. The trips check allocates five separate owners: strong corner #1, strong apex #2, strong hook #3, weak corner isolated #1 and weak hook the back. Middle safety prioritizes inside trips help and the remaining weak apex can accept a shallow #3 when physically able. A hook occupied by the back cannot accept another primary.

Ordinary Quarters retains #2 with the apex until the corresponding safety can accept its vertical release. Existing safety help stays intact until an out-to-wheel transfer actually commits. Trips Poach starts the strong safety on #2 and the strong apex on #3; weak safety may accept #3, while weak corner remains locked on isolated #1 and the middle actor owns the back. The weak corner's lack of promised deep help is explicit. Closed-side tight ends are numbered from their visible position and receive the outside corner's responsibility.

Once a receiver establishes a vertical match, subsequent cuts and stops do not erase ownership. Shallow routes are carried beyond the defender's home until a declared replacement accepts. Assignments use observed motion and blocking contact, not route definitions or hidden eligibility/blocking flags. Throws stop new transfers; confirmed carriers enter the existing coordinated run-fit/pursuit response.

## Shared transactions and fallbacks

Spot and match coverage now share one reservation/reachability/atomic-commit implementation. Transfers require actual destination position, velocity, contact and movement capability. Occupied destinations must participate in a complete exchange; bundled Cover 3 chains cannot partly commit. Manual defenders use actual movement state, retain their assignment when abandoned, and receive no automatic steering. Deep recovery uses the existing bounded interception/movement model.

Supported checks cover ordinary one/two receivers per side and three-plus-one trips with a declared back. Unsupported bunch/stack, one-sided or empty distributions, unnumbered center threats and incompatible back counts resolve before preview to named Lock Man fallbacks (Cover 1 or Cover 2 Man). The plan retains both requested and resolved identities; the UI states the actual fallback and reason. This is deliberately limited authored match coverage, not a claim that every distribution has a bespoke match check.
